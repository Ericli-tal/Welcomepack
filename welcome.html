Guide to Life Insurance

A simple summary for new Australian residents and business owners.

Understanding Protection in Australia

In Australia, Life Insurance works differently depending on how you work. Understanding this gap is the first step to security.

Scenario A: Salaried Employee

"The Safety Net"

Most standard office jobs include Default Cover. While this is a basic level of cover, you can optionally enhance this protection by buying Life Insurance outside of superannuation.

Basic cover usually automatic
Important for you
Scenario B: Small Business Owner

"The Responsibility Gap"

If you work for yourself (like Mei Zhao at her salon), you are not automatically covered. You need to actively choose Life Insurance to protect your income.

Requires active choice to protect
